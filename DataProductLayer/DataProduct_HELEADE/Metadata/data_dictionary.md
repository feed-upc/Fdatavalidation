# HELEADE Data Product — Data Dictionary

## Overview

This data product contains **synthetic data** for the HELEADE territorial data space, which brings together the managers of the wetlands of southern Alicante (El Hondo, Salinas de Santa Pola, Lagunas de La Mata y Torrevieja). It follows the HELEADE data model (see `UML.png`). It contains **intentional, deterministic quality defects**, so that the four HELEADE Data Quality Requirements (DQR1HL–DQR4HL) can be tested.

All data are generated by `Code/generate_synthetic_data.py` with a fixed random seed (`--seed 7`). The wetlands are real; the sites, coordinates, observations and operations are generated.

The three observation subclasses share `observation.csv` and are told apart by `observationType`. Columns that do not apply to a subclass are empty.

## Injected defects

| DQR    | Defect                                         | Default data | Where                                                                 |
| ------ | ---------------------------------------------- | ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| DQR1HL | A monitoring site without location             | off          | `--all-defects`: the 4th site (`WL-HONDO-S4`)                         |
| DQR2HL | Observation times not written in UTC           | **on** (5)   | `OBS-00011`, `OBS-00201`, `OBS-00334`, `OBS-00411` (`+02:00` offset); `OBS-00501` (no zone, space instead of `T`) |
| DQR3HL | `notDetected` observations reporting breeding  | off          | `--all-defects`: the first three `notDetected` observations           |
| DQR4HL | A wetland with fewer than 5 monitoring sites   | **on**       | `WL-MATA` has 4 sites (El Hondo 7, Santa Pola 6)                      |

With the default data, DQR1HL (17 of 17 sites located) and DQR3HL (0 of 79 `notDetected` observations with breeding evidence) are satisfied, and DQR2HL (505 of 510, 99.0%) and DQR4HL (smallest wetland: 4 sites) are violated. `--clean` satisfies all four; `--all-defects` violates all four.
